Tableau Studio and Tableau Data Apps change how you build. Most people will learn them the slow way: a demo here, a doc page there, an evening lost to setup.

This is the short way. Four hours live, a couple of hours between, and you come out with work running on your own site.

Every minute has to earn its place. That is the whole design.

Four weeks, two things shipped

Each session is one hour. I build it live first, then you build yours with help in the room. You leave every session with something that works.

  1. Week 01

    Build with Studio

    Ask Studio for what you want, read what it built, and check every number before anyone else sees it.

    You leave withA working dashboard, published

  2. Week 02

    Make it right

    Fix what Studio got wrong: the chart, the field, the layout. Edit in place instead of starting over, until people can read it in ten seconds.

    You leave withA dashboard you would send to your boss

  3. Week 03

    Your first Data App

    What a Data App can do that a dashboard cannot. Start one, connect it to a published data source, shape the view, and publish it.

    You leave withA Data App live on your site

  4. Week 04

    Ship it

    Everyone shows their dashboard and their app. Then how to hand it over so people actually use it, and what to build next on your own.

    You leave withBoth pieces, in use at work

What you need

I check all of this with you before week 1.

A Tableau site that has Studio

Tableau Studio needs Tableau+, Cloud+ or Server+. A trial works too.

Permission to publish

To at least one project on that site, so your work goes live, not just onto your laptop.

Data Apps turned on

A site setting. If yours is off, I tell you exactly what to ask your admin for.

Who it is for

A good fit

You already build dashboards in Tableau. You want to use Studio and Data Apps at work, not just watch a demo. You have very little spare time and want every hour to count.

Not a fit

You have never built in Tableau, or your site cannot get Studio yet. Start with the basics, or a trial, and join the next one.

Questions

When is it, and what does it cost?

The day, the time and the price go to the list first, before anyone else sees them. Join the list and you will hear before enrollment opens.

Do I need Tableau+?

You need a site with Tableau Studio, which means Tableau+, Cloud+ or Server+, or a trial. I help you check before week 1.

Do I need to code?

No. Studio does the building from what you ask for. Data Apps go further than a dashboard, and I walk you through every step of yours.

Do I bring my own data?

If you have data you can use, yes. If not, I give you a sample so you can build everything anyway.

What if I miss a session?

Every session is recorded and you get the finished files, so you can catch up on your own time and bring your questions to the next one.
