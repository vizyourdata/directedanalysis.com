# The Valuable Part — publication logo

Final mark: a Figtree-style V, mostly the light "valuable" part, with one
orange slice at the top of the right stroke (the same share as the bar's end
in the hero chart).

- `the-valuable-part-navy.*`  — light V on navy. Primary. Use on white/dark UI (Substack).
- `the-valuable-part-cream.*` — navy V on cream. Secondary, for light grounds.
  Cream must always carry the navy V; light-on-cream does not hold up.

Exports: SVG (geometry only, no fonts) plus 1024, 512 and 256 PNG.
Colors: navy #14213D, light #DDE2EA, orange #B93A1E, cream #FFF6EE.
